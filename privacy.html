Privacy policy.

The short version: your writing stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. LineType sends anonymous usage stats to help it get better, and you can turn them off in settings.

What stays on your device

Everything you make in LineType is stored only on your iPad or iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • Your handwriting: practice strokes and scores.
  • Your progress: lessons, levels, streaks, settings, and the names you give profiles.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. Images you share, like a score card, go only where you send them.

Anonymous usage stats

To learn which lessons work, which are too hard and where people stop, LineType sends a few anonymous facts about how the app is used:

  • The app was opened, and the language and plan in use (free or Pro), the theme (light or dark) and the writing hand set (left or right).
  • A lesson was finished or left: its name, how long it took, its average score, how many bonus words were written from memory, how many steps were skipped, and the step it stopped at.
  • A game was played, and its score; a language was picked; the Pro sheet was shown, and a purchase made (which plan, never payment details).
  • The app version, and whether it's an iPad or an iPhone.

Each comes with a random number made on your device when the app first sends a stat. It isn't linked to you, your Apple account or anything you do in other apps, and erasing the app's data makes a new one. These stats never include your name, profile names, your handwriting or strokes, anything you type, your contacts, your location or an advertising ID.

They are processed by TelemetryDeck, an analytics service with servers in Germany and the Netherlands that doesn't store IP addresses. We look only at totals and averages, never at a person.

To turn them off: settings (the gear) › anonymous stats › off. Nothing more is sent from then on, and everything else works the same.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No advertising or tracking, and no sharing or selling of data. The only stats are the anonymous ones above.
  • Nothing you make goes to a server: LineType works entirely offline, and anonymous stats are sent only when you're online.

Apple services the app uses

  • Purchases. LineType Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details.
  • Speech. Readings are spoken by the voices built into your device.
  • Reminders. If you turn on a daily reminder, it's a notification scheduled on your device.

If you send us feedback

"Report a bug or suggest an idea" opens an email in your own Mail app. It includes what you write, the app version, your device model and system version, and, if you choose, a screenshot of the screen you were on. Nothing is sent until you send it. We use these emails only to answer you and improve the app, and we never share them.

Children

LineType is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage stats contain nothing that identifies a person.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
